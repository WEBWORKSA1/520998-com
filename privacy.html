Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026.

520998.com (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and why.

Information you give us

When you submit a form (report request, campaign kit, contest entry, application, pledge, newsletter or contact), we receive the details you enter, such as your name, email and message. Forms are delivered to our private inbox through a third-party form-processing service (FormSubmit). We use this information only to respond, deliver what you asked for and, if you subscribed, send our newsletter. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Tools

Our calculators and decoder run in your browser. Numbers and names you type into tools are not sent to us unless you submit them in a form.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are shown a consent message through Google’s certified consent management platform before personalised ads are served.

Local storage

We store your theme choice and cookie-notice choice in your browser’s local storage.

Embedded content

Video links open YouTube; embedded players use YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you click.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and contests are limited to adults 18+.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal data via our contact page. Residents of the EU/UK (GDPR), California (CCPA/CPRA) and other regions have additional rights, which we honour.

Retention & security

We keep form messages only as long as needed to respond and for legitimate records, and we never sell personal data.

Changes

We may update this policy and will revise the date above.
