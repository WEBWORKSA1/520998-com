What does your number mean?

Phone numbers, dates, prices, licence plates, a number you keep seeing — decode it with Chinese homophones, numerology and pattern detection.

How the decoder works

Each digit has one or more Mandarin readings that sound like everyday words — 5 like 我 (I), 2 like 爱 (love), 9 like 久 (long-lasting), 8 like 发 (prosper). The decoder first looks for known multi-digit codes (the longest match wins, so 5201314 beats 520 + 1314), then reads remaining digits one by one.

The auspiciousness score weighs digits by their traditional symbolism: 8 and 9 add the most, 6 and 2 add some, 4 subtracts. The root number is the classic numerology reduction (keeping master numbers 11, 22 and 33). Patterns such as mirrors, repeats and sequences are flagged too.

For culture and fun — not a prediction or advice.
