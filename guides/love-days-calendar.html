Why Qixi moves

Qixi follows the lunisolar Chinese calendar — the 7th day of the 7th month — so its Gregorian date changes every year, usually falling in August.

Planning tip

If you only celebrate one, choose the one that means most to your partner. If you run a business, map all of them: each is a reason to talk to customers.
