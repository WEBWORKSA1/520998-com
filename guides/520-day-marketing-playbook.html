May 20 is a demand spike you can plan for. This playbook turns it into a campaign — whether you sell in China, to the Chinese diaspora, or simply want a fresh romantic moment between Valentine’s Day and summer.

Why 520 is worth a campaign

  • Built-in message: the date itself means “I love you,” so creative writes itself.
  • Proven buying behaviour: jewellery, flowers, perfume, cosmetics, dining and travel all see seasonal lifts.
  • Less crowded than Valentine’s Day in many Western markets — cheaper attention.

The 6-week timeline

WhenAction
Week −6Choose the offer, hero product and love-code price (5.20, 52.0, 520, 1314).
Week −5Brief creators; produce short video, photography and gift-wrap design.
Week −4Build landing page, bundles and gift-message options; set up tracking.
Week −3Tease on social; open an early-bird list (email/SMS/WeChat).
Week −2Launch; influencer posts; retarget visitors.
May 19–21Peak: last-minute delivery promise, digital gift cards, live sessions.
Week +1Thank-you offer, reviews, user-generated content round-up.

Offers that fit the day

  • Couples bundles at a love-code price.
  • Engraving/personalisation with 520 or 1314.
  • Gift with purchase of 520 units only — scarcity in the number itself.
  • “Say it in numbers” contest — users submit their best love code (see our contest format).

Channels

For mainland China, campaigns typically centre on WeChat, Douyin, Xiaohongshu (RED), Tmall and JD. For the diaspora and international audiences, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Pinterest and email do the heavy lifting. Localise copy — never machine-translate love messages.

Creative ideas

  1. “Decode the date” explainer video: what 520 means in 15 seconds.
  2. Packaging that hides a number code to be decoded.
  3. Countdown landing page with a love-days calendar.
  4. Customer stories of proposals on 5/21.

Measure what matters

Track incremental revenue versus the same period last year, new-customer share, average order value of bundles, and post-campaign repeat rate. Keep the creative and email list — they are reusable for Qixi and Valentine’s Day.
