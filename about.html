About

About 520998

Numbers carry feelings. 520 says “I love you.” 1314 promises “a lifetime.” 8 wishes prosperity and 4 is quietly avoided. We built 520998.com to make that language readable for everyone.

What we do

  • Explain number codes clearly and accurately, with Chinese characters and pinyin.
  • Build free, private, browser-based tools for couples, gift-givers, learners and sellers.
  • Publish practical guides on love days, etiquette and pricing.

Our name

We read 520998 as 520 (“I love you”) + 998 (“long-lasting prosperity”) — love that lasts and a life that prospers. The number is used as a domain name and descriptive code only; see our trademark disclosure.

Editorial standards

Meanings are checked against widely documented usage in Mandarin internet culture and language-learning references. Where readings vary, we say so. Numerology content is labelled as entertainment. We correct errors quickly — tell us.

How we’re funded

Advertising (Google AdSense), clearly labelled sponsorships, brand services and reader support keep every tool free.
